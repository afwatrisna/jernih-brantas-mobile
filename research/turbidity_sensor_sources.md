# Field Turbidity Sensor Research Notes

## Verified sources

| Model | Manufacturer-stated facts relevant to Jernih | Source |
|---|---|---|
| YSI EXO Turbidity Smart Sensor | Digital smart sensor with welded titanium construction and wet-mateable connectors. The listed measurement range is 0–4,000 FNU/NTU, response time is T63 under two seconds, and listed accuracy is 0.3 FNU or ±2% of reading from 0–999 FNU. It is designed to work with an EXO sonde rather than independently. | https://www.ysi.com/product/id-599101-01/exo-turbidity-smart-sensor |
| In-Situ Aqua TROLL Turbidity Sensor | Listed as an ISO 7027 nephelometric turbidity sensor with a 0–4,000 NTU range. Product documentation describes ambient-light rejection, optical compensation, low-volume calibration, and six-point factory calibration intended to support stable performance in field deployments. | https://in-situ.com/us/aqua-troll-turbidity-sensor |
| Hach 2100Q Portable Turbidimeter | A field-portable meter for manual water samples rather than unattended in-water deployment. The product page lists a 0–1,000 NTU range, assisted calibration, a two-detector system that compensates for colour, light fluctuation, and stray light, XML data transfer through an optional USB/power module, and automatic storage for up to 500 measurements. | https://www.hach.com/p-2100q-portable-turbidimeter-epa-0-1000-ntu/2100Q01 |
| Campbell Scientific OBS-3+ | A submersible, side-looking probe intended for continuous river and sediment monitoring. Its listed selectable ranges run from 0–250 to 0–4,000 NTU; listed accuracy is 2% of reading or 0.5 NTU, operating temperature is 0–40°C, maximum data rate is 10 Hz, and it works with Campbell data loggers. The manufacturer page labels it retired for new orders and points to newer alternatives, so it should be considered only for existing installations. | https://www.campbellsci.eu/obs-3plus |
| Campbell Scientific ClariVUE 10 | A current, field-deployable side-scatter probe for fixed monitoring sites. It meets ISO 7027-1:2016 and supports 0–4,000 FNU with 0.2 FNU resolution and stated accuracy of ±2% or 0.5 FNU. Its low-power SDI-12 v1.4 digital output, copper face to slow biological growth, sapphire lens, sealed optical assembly, and three-year warranty make it a strong fit for a river-station gateway with a Campbell logger or SDI-12-capable controller. | https://www.campbellsci.com/products/clarivue10 |
| YSI EXO2 + EXO Turbidity Smart Sensor | A full multiparameter sonde platform for unattended monitoring. EXO2 provides seven universal ports, a central high-torque antifouling wiper, optional depth measurement, and remote real-time integration. The compatible EXO turbidity sensor measures 0–4,000 FNU/NTU and is specified at 0.3 FNU or ±2% of reading from 0–999 FNU. This is appropriate where turbidity must be measured alongside parameters such as temperature, conductivity, dissolved oxygen, or pH. | https://www.ysi.com/exo2 and https://www.ysi.com/product/id-599101-01/exo-turbidity-smart-sensor |
